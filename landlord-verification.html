Landlord onboarding

Verify first. List after approval.

The KSh 200 listing fee does not verify a landlord. We review the owner or agent and their authority over each property before collecting the fee or opening a dashboard.

How the safety review works

1

Confirm the person.
In the live service, a reviewer checks identity and confirms the phone number with a one-time code.

2

Confirm authority for every property.
The applicant must show ownership or written permission to rent the property, such as an agent or manager authorization.

3

Check the property.
A reviewer checks that it exists and that the applicant can show the rooms they want to list.

4

Approve, pay, then create an account.
After approval, pay the KSh 200 monthly subscription, create a password and account, then open the dashboard. Each new property still needs review.

Student safety: CampusNest will not take rent deposits in this first version. Students should view the home and confirm its details before making any payment to a landlord.

Request a review

This request asks for your name, phone, area, and property authority claim so a reviewer can contact you. Do not upload ID numbers, title deeds, or tenant information. Requests are accepted only after the shared backend is configured.
